Executive Model-Choice Memo (To: Head of Retention)

[DEPLOY RECOMMENDED: LOGISTIC AT τ = 0.15]

1. Production Recommendation: Ship the standardized seven-feature Logistic Regression classifier trained via full-batch gradient descent (η = 0.1, 1,000 updates) and trigger retention outreach whenever predicted 90-day churn probability satisfies p ≥ 0.15. Pair this model with Segment C onboarding interventions and Segment B priority complaint recovery.

2. Economic Justification: Under the asymmetric loss structure where an unretained churner costs INR 400 in lost contribution margin while a proactive retention coupon costs INR 50 (an 8:1 cost asymmetry), the naive τ = 0.50 boundary incurs INR 11,800 on the held-out test set (missing 27 of 51 churners), and flagging nobody costs INR 20,400. Selecting τ* = 0.1500 strictly on the 560 training rows (training cost INR 12,150) reduces held-out test expenditure to INR 6,050—capturing 45 of 51 churners (recall 0.8824) and missing only 6.

3. Comparative Model & Ensemble Audit: At the standardized τ = 0.30 benchmark (Task 4.2), individual Logistic Regression achieves the highest F1 score (0.5714, recall 0.7451, test cost INR 7,400), outperforming Soft Voting (F1 0.5507, INR 7,650), Hard Majority Voting (F1 0.5344, INR 8,650), and 5-Nearest Neighbours (F1 0.3696, recall 0.3333, INR 14,800). Because KNN weights all seven standardized dimensions equally, four statistically noisy predictors (X4, X5, X6, X7 whose 95% Wald intervals span zero) dilute Euclidean distance neighborhoods and degrade ensemble votes.

4. Governance & Operational Risk: Across 2,000 bootstrap test resamples, τ = 0.15 maintains a 95% cost interval of [INR 4,300, INR 8,200] and outperforms τ = 0.50 in 100% of draws and across all 30 alternative stratified 70/30 splits (mean saving INR 7,417). However, if the true miss penalty falls to INR 100 (2:1 ratio), the threshold shifts to τ = 0.25 (INR 3,550 vs INR 3,700 at 0.50). Live A/B coupon redemption telemetry must be validated before full-base rollout.
