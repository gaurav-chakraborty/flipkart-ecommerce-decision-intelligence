Executive Model-Choice Memo

Recommendation: Deploy the standardized Logistic Regression model with an alert threshold of τ = 0.15, paired with Segment C retention targeting.

Financial Impact: On the 240-customer test partition (51 churners), a missed churner costs INR 400 in lost margin whereas a retention coupon costs INR 50. Lowering the threshold from the default 0.50 to τ = 0.15 reduces test operational cost from INR 11,800 to INR 6,050 (a 48.7% cost reduction) and saves INR 14,350 compared to flagging nobody (INR 20,400). At τ = 0.15, the campaign catches 45 of 51 churners (88.24% recall, missing only 6).

Why Logistic over KNN, Stump, and Ensembles: Logistic regression produces smooth calibrated probabilities required for threshold optimization. At τ = 0.50, KNN (K = 5) costs INR 14,800 (F1 = 0.3696), hard voting costs INR 8,650, and soft voting costs INR 7,650. Across 2,000 bootstrap resamples, τ = 0.15 achieves a 95% cost interval of [INR 4,300, INR 8,200] and outperforms τ = 0.50 in 100% of resamples and in 30 of 30 alternative stratified splits (mean saving INR 7,417).
